01 — Location

Studio & Location

We're based in Kempton Park and serve clients across Gauteng — both in our studio and on location.

Where are you based?

We're based in Kempton Park and proudly serve clients across Gauteng. We also offer outdoor photography and videography sessions at locations of your choice, including parks, homes, venues, and scenic outdoor locations.

What's your address?

No. 4 Fountainview, 10 Poplar Street, Kempton Park. Need directions? Just ask and we'll send you a pin location for easy navigation to our studio.

Do you offer both studio and outdoor shoots?

Yes. We offer professional studio photography sessions as well as outdoor shoots across Gauteng, depending on the style and experience you're looking for.

What happens if it rains during an outdoor shoot?

If weather conditions affect your outdoor session, we'll work with you to reschedule or move the shoot to a suitable indoor or studio location where possible.

02 — Booking

Booking & Sessions

We work strictly by appointment. A R250 deposit secures your booking, with the balance due after your shoot.

Do you accept walk-ins?

No. We work strictly by appointment to ensure each client receives a fully prepared and personalised photography experience. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred date and time.

How do I book a shoot?

We recommend booking at least 1 week in advance, although earlier bookings are always better for availability. A R250 deposit secures your studio booking, with the remaining balance due after the shoot. Need help booking? Ask us for our booking guide with full details.

Do you offer weekend bookings?

Yes, weekend bookings are available depending on availability. We recommend booking early, as weekends tend to fill up quickly.

Can I include my partner and/or children?

Yes, from Package 2 upwards. Package 1 is designed for mom alone due to the limited number of edited photos included in the package.

Do I choose the background, outfits, and poses?

Yes — with our help. Once your booking is confirmed, we'll guide you through the creative process, including outfit ideas, backgrounds, themes, and posing guidance to help you feel fully prepared and comfortable.

Can I customise my photography package?

Yes. Depending on the type of shoot, we can customise certain elements of your package to better suit your vision, style, and creative needs.

03 — Preparation

Outfits & Preparation

We have complimentary outfits available, or you're welcome to bring your own. Either way, we'll help you look your best.

Do you provide outfits?

Yes. We offer a selection of complimentary outfits, with premium dresses available for hire from R250 depending on the style and shoot type.

Can I use my own outfits?

Absolutely. You're welcome to bring your own outfits or even rent from another provider. Our wardrobe is simply there to give you extra options and help make your shoot experience easier.

Will using my own outfits affect the price?

Not at all. Our package prices exclude outfit costs. You only pay extra if you choose to rent a premium dress from us.

What should I wear for my photoshoot?

We recommend wearing outfits that make you feel comfortable and confident. Neutral colours, solid tones, and coordinated outfits usually photograph best. We're also happy to help with outfit suggestions before your session.

Do you offer makeup services?

We don't include makeup services in order to keep our packages affordable. However, we're happy to recommend trusted makeup artists located near our studio.

Can you help with posing during the shoot?

Definitely. Most of our clients are not professional models, so we guide you throughout the session with posing, expressions, and positioning to help you feel relaxed and confident in front of the camera.

04 — Delivery

Photos & Delivery

Your edited photos are delivered within 1 week via an online gallery or download link.

When will I receive my photos?

Photos are ready within 1 week. If we complete your gallery sooner, we'll send them immediately. Please note that final payment must be completed before photos are released.

How will I receive my final photos or videos?

Final edited photos and videos are delivered digitally through an online gallery or download link for quick and convenient access.

05 — Services

Our Services

From lifestyle and maternity to corporate branding and videography — we cover a wide range of photographic and visual needs.

What photography and videography services do you offer?

We specialise in maternity photography, cake smash sessions, lifestyle photography, studio photography, outdoor photography, corporate and commercial photography, branding content, and professional videography services for businesses, events, and personal milestones.

Do you offer corporate and branding photography?

Yes. We work with businesses, brands, and professionals to create high-quality corporate headshots, branding content, promotional visuals, social media content, and commercial photography.
