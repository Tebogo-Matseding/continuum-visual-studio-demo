Every cake smash session is fully styled around a theme of your choice. Browse our collection below and let us know which one speaks to you — or mix elements to create something entirely your own.

Gold crowns, pearl accents, tutu skirts or bow ties, and a castle-inspired cake. The royal treatment for your little one's first birthday.
