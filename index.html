About The Studio

Welcome to Continuum Visual Studio, a professional photography and videography studio proudly serving clients across Johannesburg, Kempton Park, and surrounding areas throughout Gauteng. Proudly backed by positive Google reviews, we specialise in corporate and commercial photography, lifestyle and private photoshoots, studio and on-location sessions, as well as professional videography services.

Studio Photography

Outdoor Photography

Natural light sessions in parks, gardens, and scenic locations across Johannesburg — perfect for families, couples, lifestyle shoots, and brand content that breathes.
