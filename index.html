About The Studio

Welcome to Continuum Visual Studio, a professional photography and videography studio based in Kempton Park, proudly serving clients across Johannesburg and surrounding areas. Proudly backed by positive Google reviews, we specialise in corporate and commercial photography, lifestyle and private photoshoots, studio and on-location sessions, as well as professional videography services.
